# PRODUCT.md — SaahVay

## What it is
SaahVay (saahvay.com) is a Shopify store selling women's clothing and complete outfits, every piece selected and labelled for specific body shapes: H (Rectangle), O (Apple), V (Inverted Triangle), X (Hourglass), A (Pear). Shape is a guidance layer on top of normal sizes.

## Unique mechanism
Every product carries the shapes it is selected for and a one-line reason. Customers enter through their shape (five cards or a 60-second quiz) and shop pieces and whole outfits chosen for it.

## Audience and scene
Women, late twenties to late fifties, busy professionals, business owners, mothers. They shop on a phone in stolen minutes (commute, after bedtime), have been burned by online returns, want guidance without judgement. Cultural home: contemporary fashion editorial, the personal stylist, the fitting room mirror.

## What the homepage must prove
Within seconds: this store knows your shape, and that removes the guessing. Primary action: Find my fit (quiz). Secondary: pick a shape card. Tertiary: shop new arrivals.

## Brand commitments (pinned by the client brief)
- Palette: black #141414, white, cream #F7F3EC, warm beige #D9C8AD, beige deep #B49E7E. No gradients, no bright accents, no gold, no large black surfaces except the footer.
- Type: Cormorant Garamond for headings (matches the logotype's Garamond character), DM Sans for everything else.
- Photography: real, diverse women, editorial, consistent crops. Generated imagery in this preview is clearly synthetic placeholder art direction to be replaced by a shoot.
- Voice: warm, direct, competent. "Selected for", never "flatters your flaws". No exclamation marks.

## Constraints
Shopify Online Store 2.0 theme on Dawn; checkout untouched; no page builders or animation libraries where CSS suffices; WCAG 2.2 AA; mobile first; reduced motion respected.

## Uninventable
Prices, reviews, testimonials, shipping and returns terms, statistics.
