      Shape, not size

      Your shape is how you are proportioned. Your size is what you buy.

      Two women can wear the same size and suit very different cuts. Shape looks at where your shoulders, waist, and hips sit relative to each other, and that is what decides whether a wrap dress, a straight trouser, or an A-line skirt will look the way you want. On SaahVay, every product carries the shapes it is selected for. You still choose your normal size on the product page.

      Shape A

      Hips wider than shoulders. Pieces that bring attention up and skim below.

      • Bring attention up with detail at the neckline and shoulder.
      • A-line and fluid skirts skim rather than cling.
      • Darker, matte fabrics below; texture and colour above.
      Read the full guide ↓

      The A shape carries its width low, which means the eye naturally lands at the hip. Everything that works for this shape does one of two things: it draws the eye upward, or it lets fabric fall cleanly from the widest point instead of gripping it. Boat necks, structured shoulders, and detail at the collarbone do the first job. A-line skirts, fluid wide legs, and wrap dresses that tie above the hip do the second.

      Common questions

      Is body shape the same as my clothing size?
      No. Shape is about proportion: where your shoulders, waist, and hips sit relative to each other. Size is the label you buy. Every product on this page still has a normal size selector, and the size guide explains how each piece is measured.
      What if I am between two shapes?
      Many women are. Start with the shape the quiz ranks first, then look at the pieces tagged for both. The "Also works for" label on each product shows the second shape it suits.
      What if a piece does not work on me?
      Shape recommendations are styling guidance, not a guarantee. If something does not work, our returns page explains the current policy and how to start a return.

        Our story

        We built the store we wanted to shop in.

        SaahVay started with a familiar frustration: a screen full of clothes and no way to tell which of them would work on you. Size charts answer whether something will close. They do not answer whether it will look the way you hoped.

        So every piece here is selected for specific body shapes and says so. Complete outfits take the guesswork out of what goes with what. Fit notes are honest, model sizes are listed, and nothing on this site is ever called a flaw.

        What we stand behind

        Chosen for your shape

        Every product names the shapes it is selected for and gives a one-line reason. That is the promise the store is built on.

        Outfits, not just items

        Complete combinations remove the hardest part of shopping: deciding what goes with what.

        Honest fit

        Real measurements, model sizes, plain language about fabric and stretch, and a returns policy stated the way it actually works.

        We want getting dressed to be the easy part of your day.

        Whether you are heading into a meeting, a school run, or a weekend that is finally yours, you should be able to open SaahVay, find what suits you in minutes, and feel like yourself in it.
